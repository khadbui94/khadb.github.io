About Me

Who I am

My name is Kha Bui. I am an aspiring web developer studying Computer Science. I am a senior at the University of California, Irvine. I grew up in Viet Nam and moved to the United States a few years ago.

Before coming to college, I had no experience with coding and web design. I went to Orange Coast College in 2013 and began my passion in Computer Science. It was harder than I thought, but I really enjoyed coding even though I wasn’t very good at it. I started practicing and refining my skills. Later on, I transferred to UCI for my Bachelor of Science degree. I began going deeper into the major, studying for classes, and working on my side projects. I have worked to develop partnership and leadership through many group projects and activities that I joined. I have completed courses from basic coding to operating systems and web development.

Why are you here?

There are many reasons you might be visiting my portfolio. You may be a recruiter looking for a team member, a friend, or just someone who found something interesting here. Whoever you are, I hope this portfolio helps you learn a little about me. Enjoy!
